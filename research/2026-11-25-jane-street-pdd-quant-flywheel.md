# PDD — Jane Street Quant Trading Desk and Quant Flywheel
Timestamp: 2026-11-25 CST
## Official evidence and role distinctions
Jane Street groups trading, research and ML together. Quantitative traders identify signals and pricing inefficiencies, develop models, execute and improve strategies, manage risk and explore new products. Most trading is automated; trader responsibility is not merely manual execution. QR emphasizes experimental design, datasets, time series, features, model validation and research-to-production. Boundaries overlap intentionally; Chinese firms' titles also vary and are not universally interchangeable.
Software engineering includes research tooling, production trading systems (market data, orders, exchange connectivity, risk, latency), platform engineering and ML infrastructure. ML researchers build and test models; ML engineers typically focus on training/deployment and computational performance (analytical distinction, not a verified internal job matrix). TDOE supports infrastructure, operational workflows, queries, clearing and settlement coordination.
## Illustrative desk case (not proprietary)
ETF fair value 100, bid 99.98, ask 100.02. QR estimates fair value and validates signals; QT manages quotes, inventory, hedging and live strategy performance; SWE delivers reliable execution; ML engineering improves modeling pipelines; TDOE manages operational exceptions. Spread is not guaranteed profit.
## Quant Flywheel
Capital + data + compute + talent → collaborative desk (QR/QT/SWE/ML/TDOE) → research, models, execution and improvements → potential net trading profit → reinvestment in people, data, compute and capital. Improvement Flywheel and Capital Flywheel reinforce each other conditionally: account for losses, market impact, risk limits, capacity, costs and diminishing returns. This is a conceptual business model, not confirmed internal capital allocation. Quant Library is the knowledge base for the framework.
## Research interests
Learning & Decision Intelligence (ML/DL/RL, mathematics, statistics, prediction and pattern recognition) and Scalable AI Systems (GPU, training, serving, high-performance systems) overlap strongly with quant research. Human-AI & Multimodal Intelligence may support research workflows. Corporate finance is complementary domain knowledge. Quant research requires empirical validation; no claim ML dominates all trading approaches.
